## Where to Search Research Papers

### General and free
| Website | Link | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| Google Scholar | https://scholar.google.com | Searching across all publishers, citation counts, "cited by" |
| Semantic Scholar | https://www.semanticscholar.org | Search with short AI summaries, finds related papers |
| arXiv | https://arxiv.org | Free preprints in robotics, mechanics, control, materials |
| CORE | https://core.ac.uk | Free full-text open-access papers |
| DOAJ | https://doaj.org | Directory of open-access journals |
| ResearchGate | https://www.researchgate.net | Papers shared by authors, can message authors |
| Unpaywall | https://unpaywall.org | Finds free legal copies of paywalled papers |
| Connected Papers | https://www.connectedpapers.com | Visual map of related papers |

### Search tips
- Use specific keywords, for example `"suspension design" agricultural robot` instead of just `robot`.
- Use quotes for exact phrases and add `review` to find survey papers.
- Start with a **review paper**, then follow its references.
- Sort by year to find recent work, and by citations to find the important papers.
- If a paper is paywalled, try Unpaywall, the author's page, or your college library login.
- Save every useful paper in the Research Papers table with a tag.
